# Loc’din by Kosi
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Confirmed: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase Postgres/Auth/Storage. Local development first; proposed deployment is Vercel. User will provision Supabase.

## Users
Nigerian and international customers buying handmade loc extensions, and the brand owner managing catalogue, orders, and custom enquiries.

## Product Purpose
Help customers choose their loc style, understand what a bundle contains, configure a valid variant, and purchase with clear shipping and charge-currency information. Custom work starts with a quotation enquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Confirmed 1 November 2026: Nigeria and international checkout are in scope, NGN is the base currency, made-to-order products and custom quotations are supported. International presentation should be moderate and consistent with the overall UI. Guest checkout; invited administrator access.

## Brand Commitments
Retain the provided logo and the WhatsApp reference's near-black, ivory, copper identity, editorial typography, and loc-focused photography. No redesign workshop is required; the supplied reference and approved brief establish the direction.

## Open Configuration
Real sellable products, quantities, prices, lead times, original photography, legal policies, contact details, approved payment provider, merchant international acceptance, destination shipping rates, and additional settlement currencies. Development seed data and imagery must be labelled illustrative. No live charge without approved configuration.

## Product Principles
- Make product and bundle details legible before asking for a purchase.
- Treat international buyers as part of the ordinary shopping journey.
- Never disguise estimates as actual charge amounts or shipping promises.
- Keep personal data and commerce mutations behind verified permissions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive web interface with WCAG 2.2 AA as the implementation target, keyboard interaction, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and country-appropriate address inputs.
