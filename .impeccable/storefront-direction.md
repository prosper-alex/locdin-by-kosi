# Storefront direction contract

Mode: Persuade on the homepage; Operate for configuration, cart, checkout, enquiries and administration.

Authority: the user's WhatsApp reference, supplied logo, approved implementation brief, and explicit request for a moderate UI with full international checkout support. The direction and layout are pinned, so no competing-world workshop or generated UI comp is needed. Generated assets supply photographs only; no generated interface is treated as approved design.

First viewport: a near-black/copper header, an editorial portrait on the left, large ivory serif headline and restrained copper italic on the right. One solid shopping action and one quiet custom-order action. Mobile places copy before the portrait to keep the proposition and action immediately available.

Narrative: handmade individuality, style collections, tactile craftsmanship, selected products, a personal-design invitation. Real claims only; photographs, catalogue amounts and shipping figures are expressly illustrative until approved.

Signature interaction: the destination/currency panel joins the ordinary navigation and footer; its preference carries through catalogue, bag and checkout. The final charge currency remains explicit. A short hero portrait/headline entrance and copper accent-line animation introduce the brand. Photo crops and action arrows give restrained hover/focus feedback; all decorative spatial motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

International UI: one destination control, country-appropriate address labels, destination-specific shipping, distinct production/transit estimates, and a short duties/currency explanation at the decision point. No flag wall, currency dashboard, or noisy international banner.

Quality bar: readable body text and fine warm borders; consistent 1280px content measure; responsive images; no placeholder icon controls; options and quantities have accessible labels and states; all visible navigation resolves.

Constraints: no live claims or charges from preview data. No credentials are present. Preserve Grok's original mark and all supplied source assets. Test local frontend and the PostgreSQL migration; report provider/cloud verification separately.

Typography and spacing refinement approved 1 November 2026: warm Lora editorial headlines, DM Sans body/shopping content, 16px ordinary body copy, readable supporting roles, larger local grouping gaps and 112/88/72px desktop/tablet/mobile section rhythm. Exact implemented rules are in DESIGN.md.
